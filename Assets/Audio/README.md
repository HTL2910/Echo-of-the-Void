# Audio Specifications (Spec §7)

## Music Tracks & Stems (Spec §7.1)
- **Zone 1 (Ruins of Aethelgard):**
  - **BPM:** 110 BPM
  - **Time Signature:** 4/4
  - **Loop Length:** 8 bars = 32 beats (17.4545s)
  - **Prime Stem:** `Assets/Audio/Music/MUS_Z1_Prime.ogg` (Acoustic Piano arpeggios, Bowed Cello bass, Clockwork Mechanical Percussion)
  - **Echo Stem:** `Assets/Audio/Music/MUS_Z1_Echo.ogg` (16th-note Synth Arpeggiator, 808 Sub-bass, Shimmer Granular Reverb)

- **Title / Menu:**
  - `Assets/Audio/Music/MUS_Menu.ogg` (Atmospheric void drone with melodic piano motif, 16.0s loop)
